Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

HomeKeep is an Android app that helps you keep a register of household assets, receipts, warranties, repairs and maintenance reminders. This policy explains what is stored and why. It applies to the HomeKeep app and this website.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a password hash, used to sign you in and keep your records private. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth; the app itself never stores your password.
  • Content you create: asset details (including serial numbers, purchase dates and prices), repair history, reminders, notes, and any files you choose to upload such as receipts, warranty PDFs, manuals and photos.
  • Technical data: standard request logs kept by our hosting/database provider for security and reliability. The app does not collect advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, SMS, health or financial account data.

Why we process it

Only to provide the app: authenticating you, storing and syncing your records, showing reminders, and letting you delete your data. We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a Supabase project (Postgres database and object storage) configured by the app operator. Requests use HTTPS. Files live in a private storage bucket and each account can only read or write its own folder. Row Level Security policies on every table restrict rows to the signed-in user.

Retention and deletion

Your records are kept until you delete them. You can delete an individual asset together with its files at any time. You can delete your entire account and all associated data from Settings → Delete my account and data. Deletion removes your assets, attachments, repairs, reminders, stored files and the authentication record. See the account deletion page for details. Some provider backups may retain copies for a short period before expiring.

Sharing

Data is not shared with third parties except the infrastructure providers that operate the app (Supabase for database, authentication and file storage, and Google Play for distribution). These providers process data on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by law.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Access to records and files is restricted per account by database policies. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your device and account password protected.

Children

HomeKeep is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above will be updated and the new version published at this address.

Contact
