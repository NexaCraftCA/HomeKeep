Delete your HomeKeep account and data

Fastest way, inside the app: open HomeKeep, go to Settings (gear icon on the asset list), then tap Delete my account and data and confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address and authentication record)
  • All assets, including serial numbers, purchase details and notes
  • All uploaded receipts, warranty documents, manuals and photos
  • All repair history and maintenance reminders

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Nothing is kept for marketing or analytics. Short-lived provider backups may expire on their normal schedule.

If you cannot use the app

Email the address associated with the account, or open an issue, and ask for account deletion. Requests are actioned within 30 days. Please send the request from the account email so ownership can be confirmed.

Delete individual records

You do not need to delete your whole account to remove data. Delete an asset from its detail page to remove the asset along with its files, repairs and reminders. Remove individual files with the delete icon next to each document.
