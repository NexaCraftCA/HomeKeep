HomeKeep

A quiet home asset register for Android

Keep the details that are painful to find later: serial numbers, receipts, warranty end dates and where the thing actually lives in the house. Log repairs and keep an eye on upkeep without turning your home into a spreadsheet.

What it does
  • Assets with category, brand, model, serial, location, price and warranty
  • Receipts, warranty documents, manuals and photos
  • Repair and service history with vendor and cost
  • Maintenance reminders with optional repeat, plus daily notifications
  • Private, per-account storage; delete everything from inside the app
